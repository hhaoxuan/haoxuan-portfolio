The Chess Initiative

About

Haoxuan in 8th grade

Founding of TCI

I founded The Chess Initiative around February of 2025,
in my freshmen year of high school. Shortly after the
collapse of the main chess organization in our area,
Chess4All, I wanted to try and take on the torch.
From there, the TCI team built this organization from the
ground up.

Our Mission

Our primary mission is to spread the love of chess in
the San Gabriel Valley. Growing up, I loved playing
chess around and everything associated with it. I
want my community to love the game as much as I do.
We attempt to achieve this through frequent posts
and in-person tournaments.

Personal Ventures

The Chess Initiative has been a tremendous aid
to my skill set. It has inspired me to take on an
active leadership role, coordinating with other
founders to accomplish our mission. I have polished
my skills in communication,graphic design, and video
editing from these experiences.

Projects

Vertical
Graphics

Tournament Attendance Graphic

Guess why I picked this one?
That's right, it's the only vertical one here.
This graphic was my first experiment with grids on a
vertical canvas. The overlapping pictures and text is
inspired by one of NIKE's (or ADIDAS, upon second
thought) advertisements. I enjoy how this graphic gets
its message across very quickly, yet maintains a kind
of sophistication.

Tournament Graphics

Thankgiving Graphic

This graphic was made for an interscholastic
tournament co-hosted by TCI on 11/23/2025.
Although this is a very simplistic visual, I
attribute this graphic as one of the catalysts
which influenced my current design style.

January Tournament Graphic

This graphic was for another interscholastic
tournament hosted on 1/25/26. It was my first
time using a column grid. Fun fact: I started
working on this project roughly a month or two
before the actual event, then finalized it after
I received notice about the tournament.

Endgame Essentials

Opposition Graphic

The first addition to the endgame essentials
series, where we teach endgame concepts through
graphics. This graphic was one of my more
experimental ones, inspired by the style of swiss
punk. However I integrated my own style into it,
creating a unique blend of the two.

Zugzwang Graphic

Similar to the opposition graphic, this one
is also very experimental. Oddly, this was
created due to my frustration with a creative
block, leading to a more aggressive style than
the normal graphics. Overall, the graphic was
a fun joust into more punk-inspired design.

Events Hosted

2025 Thanksgiving Interscholastic Tournament

Almansor Park (Venue) • 30 Players (Attendance) • Brandon Cong (Winner)

This was our first interscholastic tournament with Arcadia,
Mark Keppel, Alhambra, and Schurr High School (local schools
in the San Gabriel Valley). By coordinating with these high
school's club presidents, I gained a newfound appreciation
for event management and planning. We used google sheets for
tracking and pairing the tournament, which could be streamlined
later on. The graphic for promoting this event was done by me
(Thanksgiving Graphic).

2026 February Open

Almansor Park • 10 Players • Haoxuan Huang

This was TCI's third interscholastic tournament with Arcadia,
Mark Keppel, Alhambra, and Schurr High School. We were
gradually seeing the number of attendees decrease, and knew
that we had to differentiate in order to achieve our mission.
This tournament had a decent turnout, but we plan on hosting
more tournaments outside of the high school “coalition” we've
created. The promotional graphic was made by me.

2026 Spring Workshop & Tournament

The Dream School House • 28 Kids Total • 5 Placements

This event originally stemmed from an outreach message I sent
in mid-December 2025. Upon interest in the event from
TheDreamSchoolHouse, we scheduled and created the workshop
along with the tournament from scratch, enlisting help from
Mark Keppel High School for managing the physical events.
This was our first event in collaboration with an organization
outside the local high schools. Promotion graphics were
created by me.

The Chess Initiative

About

Haoxuan in 8th grade

Founding of TCI

I founded The Chess Initiative around February of 2025,
in my freshmen year of high school. Shortly after the
collapse of the main chess organization in our area,
Chess4All, I wanted to try and take on the torch.
From there, the TCI team built this organization from the
ground up.

Our Mission

Our primary mission is to spread the love of chess around
the San Gabriel Valley. Growing up, I loved playing chess and
everything associated with it. I want my community to love
the game as much as I do. We attempt to achieve this
through frequent posts and in-person tournaments.

Personal Ventures

The Chess Initiative has been a tremendous aid to my skill set.
It has inspired me to take on an active leadership role,
coordinating with other founders to accomplish our
mission. I have polished my skills in communication, graphic
design, and video editing from these experiences.

Projects

Vertical
Graphics

Tournament Attendance Graphic

Guess why I picked this one?
That's right, it's the only vertical one here.
This graphic was my first experiment with grids on a
vertical canvas. The overlapping pictures and text is
inspired by one of NIKE's (or ADIDAS, upon second
thought) advertisements. I enjoy how this graphic gets
its message across very quickly, yet maintains a kind
of sophistication.

Tournament Graphics

Thankgiving Graphic

This graphic was made for an interscholastic
tournament co-hosted by TCI on 11/23/2025.
Although this is a very simplistic visual, I
attribute this graphic as one of the catalysts
which influenced my current design style.

January Tournament Graphic

This graphic was for another interscholastic
tournament hosted on 1/25/26. It was my first
time using a column grid. Fun fact: I started
working on this project roughly a month or two
before the actual event, then finalized it after
I received notice about the tournament.

Endgame Essentials

Opposition Graphic

The first addition to the endgame essentials
series, where we teach endgame concepts through
graphics. This graphic was one of my more
experimental ones, inspired by the style of swiss
punk. However I integrated my own style into it,
creating a unique blend of the two.

Zugzwang Graphic

Similar to the opposition graphic, this one
is also very experimental. Oddly, this was
created due to my frustration with a creative
block, leading to a more aggressive style than
the normal graphics. Overall, the graphic was
a fun joust into more punk-inspired design.
